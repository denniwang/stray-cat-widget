A little cat that tells you to rest your eyes.

Stray Cat lives in your Mac's menu bar. It pops into a corner of your screen every now and then, and once an hour it asks you to look away.

Download for macOS
Free · macOS 13+ · Apple Silicon & Intel · latest version

Pop-up cat

Peeks in from a bottom corner at random times, then wanders off. Click it to send it away.

Gentle warning

A 15-second countdown card in the corner, with Skip and Snooze. Nothing dims until it ends.

Real break

The screen dims over everything, full-screen apps included, with a countdown and a random cat or GIF.

Your pictures

Add your own images and GIFs, or pull a random GIF from a Tenor search such as “aura”.

Screen-share safe

Stays hidden while you share on Zoom or are on a call, so it never interrupts a meeting.

Yours to tune

Interval, break length, dimming, snooze and start-at-login, all in one small settings window.

Install

Paste this into Terminal. It downloads the latest version into Applications and opens it, with no security warning:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/denniwang/stray-cat-widget/main/install.sh | bash

Use Homebrew? Run this instead:

brew tap denniwang/stray-cat-widget https://github.com/denniwang/stray-cat-widget
brew install --cask denniwang/stray-cat-widget/straycat

Prefer the download button? The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS may say it is “damaged” or can't verify it. That's the quarantine flag on downloaded files, not a real problem. Run this once, then open the app:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/StrayCat.app

Screen-share detection is best-effort: it recognises Zoom shares and any app using the microphone, but not every screen-sharing tool.
